# Choose behavior by the job

These are prompt-design patterns, not customer configurations or factual templates. The live
server supplies schema/examples. Read each selected business's approved sources for its facts.

| Business | Conversation job | Evidence and boundaries to test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dental practice | Explain published services and guide an appointment enquiry | Published service/price; missing insurance detail; language; no diagnosis; no false appointment confirmation |
| Auto service | Clarify the vehicle issue and the workshop enquiry | Vehicle/context already given; published service terms; no invented repair quote or diagnosis; calendar connection before promising a booking |
| Padel club | Explain courts, training and membership, then qualify interest | Published pricing and opening terms; player/group needs; no invented court availability or booking |
| Sailing school | Help choose a course using experience, dates and purpose | Course/location-specific terms; supported credentials; seasonal schedule; no invented departure or availability |
| Legal/document service | Clarify document, jurisdiction, purpose and deadline | Correct country-specific source; published process and cost; no guaranteed official outcome or unsupported legal advice |
| AgentDeck | Explain the product and identify a useful business case | Published offering; price scope; unknown integration; honest handoff; no promise that setup or a callback already happened |

Source-approved examples from existing agents can inform tone and conversation structure during
private development. Never put their unpublished contact details, full prompts or private
knowledge files into this public repository. The live-site evaluation catalogue uses public URLs;
mock cases use explicitly synthetic material and cannot establish real-business readiness.
